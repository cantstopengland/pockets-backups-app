Privacy policy

Pockets backups is a personal tool, used only by its owner.

  • It asks Google only for access to files it creates itself (the drive.file scope). It cannot see or change anything else in Google Drive.
  • The only files it creates are backup copies of the owner's own database, saved in the owner's own Google Drive.
  • It does not collect, share or sell any information, and sends nothing to anyone other than Google Drive.
  • Access can be removed at any time from your Google Account, under Security → Third-party apps & services.
